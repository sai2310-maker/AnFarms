True country chicken cannot be rushed inside cages. At AN Farms, our native breeds—including Desi Natu Kodi, Pure Aseel, and Black-Meat Kadaknath—spend their days running across open pastures, scratching the earth for insects, eating fresh Moringa (Munaga) leaves, and basking in natural sunlight.

Pure Farm Dairy & Traditional Vedic Bilona Ghee

Commercial packet milk goes through homogenization, heavy pasteurization, and days of cold-storage transit. At AN Farms, our native cows and Murrah buffaloes graze on organic green fodder grown on our own land.

Calf-First Ethical Milking

The calf always drinks its full share first. Happy, stress-free mothers produce naturally sweet, nutrient-rich milk.

Eco-Friendly Glass Bottles Within 3 Hours

Milked at 4:00 AM, chilled immediately to 4°C to prevent bacterial growth naturally, and delivered in sanitized glass bottles.

Hand-Churned Clay Pot Bilona Ghee

We boil whole milk on a slow firewood stove, set curd in earthen pots, churn makkhan at dawn, and simmer into granular golden ghee.

The Heart Behind AN Farms

"We Don't Run a Factory. We Nurture a Living Ecosystem."

"When I started AN Farms in Hyderabad, I saw how families were forced to choose between adulterated packet milk and commercial broiler chicken pumped with antibiotics. Our goal was to return to the roots—where cattle graze under open skies, country chickens forage naturally in orchards, and food acts as natural medicine."

Under Venkat Yanamala’s hands-on supervision, AN Farms practices zero-waste circular farming. Organic cow dung from our dairy barn is converted into rich vermicompost, which nourishes our pesticide-free green fodder plots and fruit trees—creating a clean, natural habitat for our native poultry flocks.
